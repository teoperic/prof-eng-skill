## How to use
1. Read the whole question once. Note what it gives and what it asks for.
2. Find the row below whose left column matches what the question asks.
3. Open the linked card and follow its **Steps**. The card is self-contained.
4. Check the card's **Pitfalls** before you write the final answer.
5. No row fits? Use "By lecture" to find the lecture that covers the topic, then "When stuck".
6. Types are ordered by how often they appeared in past exams: the top rows are the most likely questions.

## When stuck
- Write down every given value with its unit, and what is asked.
- Look for the quantity that is asked for in the Formula sections of the cheatsheets.
- Try a limiting case (zero, infinity, symmetric case) to check a result.
- Move on and come back: points are per question.
